About MIS 2026

The IEEE Solid-State Circuits and Microwave India Summit (MIS) 2026 is a landmark high-impact event bringing together visionaries from industry, academia, defense, and research to advance India's RF and microwave ecosystem.

Hosted at KIIT Deemed to be University, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, this two-day summit serves as India's premier platform for microwave and RF technology exchange, featuring distinguished keynote speakers, presidential addresses from four IEEE Societies (SSCS, MTT-S, AESS, and AP-S), an industry panel discussion, and industry exhibition stalls.

Aim: The Conclave aims to establish a strong platform for interaction and collaboration between academia, industry, researchers, and students in emerging areas of RF, microwave, semiconductor, and antenna technologies.

About IEEE MTT-S

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) is one of the largest and most active technical societies within the IEEE, with over 11,000 members worldwide. Founded in 1952, MTT-S is dedicated to advancing the theory and applications of RF, microwave, millimeter-wave, and terahertz technologies across fields including communications, sensing, medical applications, defense, and space.

MTT-S publishes premier journals, organizes flagship conferences such as IMS and MWC, and supports a global network of chapters, student branches, and technical committees. Through its Members and Geographic Activities (MGA) programs, MTT-S actively fosters regional communities of engineers and researchers across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and the Americas.

IEEE MTT-S Region 10 represents the Asia-Pacific region, and India is one of the most vibrant and growing hubs within this network — making Bhubaneswar the ideal host city for the inaugural IEEE Solid-State Circuits and Microwave India Summit.

About KIIT University

Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) is a Deemed-to-be University located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. Established in 1992 by Dr. Achyuta Samanta, KIIT has grown into one of India's premier technical and research institutions, recognized by NAAC with an 'A++' grade and ranked consistently among the top engineering universities in India.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, over 30,000 students, and a strong focus on research and innovation, KIIT provides an ideal setting for international academic conferences. Its Campus-12, Campus-6, and Central Library venues offer world-class facilities for hosting MIS 2026, supported by the university's School of Electronics Engineering — a hub of research in embedded systems, signal processing, and communication technologies.

3MT Competition

The 3 Minute Thesis (3MT) Competition provides MTech and PhD researchers the opportunity to communicate the significance of their research to a non-specialist audience in just three minutes — enhancing communication skills, encouraging interdisciplinary engagement, and showcasing innovative work.

Eligibility
  • Open to MTech and PhD students at recognized universities/institutions
  • Research must relate to: Microwave Engineering · RF and Communication Systems · Antennas and Propagation · Radar Systems · Aerospace and Defense Electronics · Semiconductor Technologies · Signal Processing and AI for RF/Microwave · Emerging Deep-Tech Areas
  • Participants must submit abstract (Google Form) and proof of affiliation
Competition Format
Preliminary Round (Online)
  • 250-word research abstract
  • Single presentation slide
  • Short biography (100 words)
  • Evaluated on novelty, impact, and clarity
  • Top participants shortlisted for final round
Final Round (At MIS 2026 — 26 Sep)
  • 3-minute presentation or less
  • One static slide only
  • No animations, videos, or props
  • Mixed audience: academia, industry, students
Rules
  • Max presentation time: 3 minutes (exceeding = disqualification)
  • One static slide allowed
  • Language: English
  • Must cover: Research problem · Methodology · Key findings · Societal/industrial relevance
